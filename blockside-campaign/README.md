## Brand quick reference

- **Wordmark:** BLOCKSIDE — bold extended geometric sans, ALL CAPS, one word. "BLOCK" in white + "SIDE" in golden-orange gradient, thin coastal-blue keyline underline. Minimum clear space = logo height.
- **Headline type:** ultra-bold condensed grotesque, ALL CAPS (free options: Anton, Archivo Black, Bebas Neue).
- **Subtitle type:** clean geometric sans (Archivo, Inter), ALL CAPS, +20% letter-spacing.
- **Palette:** Deep Navy `#0A1626` · Coastal Blue `#1E5F8C` · Sky Aqua `#4FB3D9` · Sunset Orange `#FF6A2B` · Gold `#FFC24B` · Warm Sand `#E8D5B5` · Ink `#05090F`
- **Grade — "Golden Coast":** teal-and-orange cinematic grade; warm highlights, navy shadows; soft haze; subtle grain; gentle vignette.
- **Layout law:** headlines lower-left on a fixed baseline grid (hero banners 1 & 10: lower-center-left); wordmark bottom-left (1 & 10: top-center); "IN DEVELOPMENT" pill bottom-right on every banner.
- **Format:** 16:9, 1920×1080, 96 px safe margins.

## Production routes (pick one per banner)

1. **AI generation (recommended):** paste the matching prompt from `03-image-prompts.md`, generate a clean art plate (no baked text), then composite typography per the overlay spec in Canva/Photoshop/Figma. Guarantees perfect, readable text.
2. **Designer brief:** hand `02-banner-designs.md` to an artist — it is written as a full art brief.
3. **Hybrid:** generate plates, clean up artifacts (hands, signage), add type in post.

---

## Honesty rule (campaign-wide)

All imagery is **concept art / development visuals**. Never label it as gameplay or a screenshot. Keep the "IN DEVELOPMENT" badge on every banner and use the phrase "development visual" in captions.
