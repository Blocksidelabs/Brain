# BLOCKSIDE — 10 Banner Designs (Art Direction)

## Shared design system — applies to every banner

- **Format:** 16:9, 1920×1080, 96 px safe margins. Landscape only.
- **Look:** cinematic AAA open-world key art; photoreal environments; realistic materials; strong depth and atmosphere. No UI, no low-poly, no floating vehicles, no cyberpunk/neon-wireframe styling, no gibberish text.
- **Grade — "Golden Coast":** warm golden highlights, teal-blue shadows, deep navy base, soft atmospheric haze, subtle film grain, gentle vignette.
- **Typography lockup**
  - Headline: ultra-bold condensed grotesque, ALL CAPS, white (Anton / Archivo Black / Bebas Neue), 96–140 px, anchored lower-left on a fixed baseline grid; soft black shadow (0/4/16 px @ 60%).
  - Subtitle (where used): clean geometric sans, ALL CAPS (Archivo / Inter), gold `#FFC24B`, ≈40% of headline size, +20% tracking.
  - Wordmark: **BLOCK** (white) + **SIDE** (gold gradient) with a thin coastal-blue keyline underline; bottom-left (banners 1 & 10: top-center). Clear space ≥ logo height.
  - Badge: small "IN DEVELOPMENT" pill, navy 80% fill, gold text, bottom-right — on every banner.
- **Text scrim:** lower-left gradient (transparent → `#05090F` @ 65%) so type stays readable on bright art.

## BANNER 1 — THE CITY IS YOURS (pairs with Tweet 1)

- **Visual:** breathtaking panoramic coastal American city at golden hour — palm-lined boulevard curving from leafy hills toward a glass-and-art-deco downtown; crescent beach and calm ocean on the right; marina; long shadows; layered haze.
- **Camera:** high aerial, 24 mm, slight downward tilt; skyline on the upper-right third.
- **Lighting:** low warm backlight; rim-lit towers; subtle bloom.
- **Typography:** hero layout — wordmark top-center; headline **THE CITY IS YOURS.** (largest of the campaign) lower-center-left; subtitle **A NEW OPEN WORLD IS TAKING SHAPE.** in gold beneath.
- **Notes:** flagship frame; also crop for the X profile header.

## BANNER 2 — EXPLORE THE CITY (pairs with Tweet 2)

- **Visual:** street-level cinematic view of a sunlit commercial avenue — pastel art-deco storefronts, awnings, sidewalk cafés, pedestrians, palm planters; the city's tallest tower terminates the street.
- **Camera:** 35 mm eye level, slight low angle, one-point perspective.
- **Lighting:** warm mid-morning rake light; warm facades, cool shade.
- **Typography:** headline **ONE CITY. ENDLESS POSSIBILITIES.** lower-left. No subtitle.
- **Notes:** keep sidewalk detail crisp — this banner sells "walkability."

## BANNER 3 — LIVE YOUR OWN STORY (pairs with Tweet 3)

- **Visual:** third-person, over-the-shoulder view: original character (coral open shirt, white tee, rolled jeans, sneakers — fully original design) on the right third of a sidewalk, gazing across a busy street toward market stalls and string lights.
- **Camera:** 50 mm, chest height behind the character's right shoulder; shallow depth of field, sharp mid-ground.
- **Lighting:** warm morning sun; soft bounce off pavement.
- **Typography:** headline **YOUR WORLD. YOUR STORY.** lower-left.
- **Notes:** the character must read as original, not evocative of existing franchises; captions must say "concept."

## BANNER 4 — GET BEHIND THE WHEEL (pairs with Tweet 4)

- **Visual:** original-design modern sports coupe (sunset-orange metallic, no real-world badges) sweeping along a palm-lined coastal boulevard at sunset; ocean left; background motion-blurred for speed while the car stays tack-sharp; sunset reflections in the paint.
- **Camera:** low front three-quarter tracking angle, 35 mm, near asphalt.
- **Lighting:** golden rim light, warm bounce, long shadows.
- **Typography:** headline **FIND YOUR RIDE. OWN THE ROAD.** lower-left.
- **Notes:** wheels and paint must hold up in close-up — car shots expose weaknesses.

## BANNER 5 — A LIVING CITY (pairs with Tweet 5)

- **Visual:** populated intersection from an elevated three-quarter vantage — sedans, hatchbacks, a yellow cab and a city bus at realistic spacing at the lights; pedestrians mid-crosswalk; corner café with awning; late-afternoon warm light.
- **Camera:** 28 mm from a second-floor balcony vantage, slight downward angle.
- **Typography:** headline **THE CITY NEVER STANDS STILL.** lower-left.
- **Notes:** pairs with the transparency tweet — keep the systems-in-progress framing honest.

## BANNER 6 — EVERY HOUR CHANGES THE CITY (pairs with Tweet 6)

- **Visual:** one continuous panoramic skyline where lighting transitions across the frame — dawn pink over the marina (left) → bright noon downtown (center) → night with lit windows, street lamps and headlight streaks (right).
- **Camera:** elevated 24 mm panorama, level horizon.
- **Lighting:** smooth, believable color-temperature gradient left to right; the frame must read as one shot, not a collage.
- **Typography:** headline **EVERY HOUR TELLS A DIFFERENT STORY.** lower-left.
- **Notes:** the signature "time" frame of the campaign.

## BANNER 7 — WEATHER CHANGES EVERYTHING (pairs with Tweet 7)

- **Visual:** dramatic rainy dusk street — wet asphalt mirroring headlights, traffic lights and storefront glow; a taxi approaching through drizzle; fog softening the block; umbrella pedestrians; cool blue-gray base with warm amber pools.
- **Camera:** 35 mm near road level, shallow depth of field, bokeh highlights.
- **Typography:** headline **FEEL EVERY CHANGE.** lower-left.
- **Notes:** most atmospheric frame; use the scrim generously — reflections are busy behind text.

## BANNER 8 — WHAT'S COMING NEXT? (pairs with Tweet 8)

- **Visual:** blue-hour overlook — an original-design muscle coupe at a scenic coastal turnoff, headlights aimed along a winding cliffside highway toward a glittering downtown across the bay; first stars; "the road ahead" as a development-roadmap motif.
- **Camera:** 24 mm from just behind and above the car; city in the upper two-thirds.
- **Typography:** headline **WE'RE JUST GETTING STARTED.** lower-left; subtitle **VEHICLES • AI CITY LIFE • MISSIONS • PERFORMANCE** in small gold caps (roadmap teaser).
- **Notes:** the subtitle doubles as the visual roadmap — update it as the real roadmap evolves.

## BANNER 9 — BUILT FOR OPEN-WORLD FREEDOM (pairs with Tweet 9)

- **Visual:** epic high aerial panorama — crescent beach with tiny believable figures, palm promenade and pastel hotels, elevated coastal highway with light traffic, sailboats on turquoise water, downtown rising behind; late-afternoon clarity.
- **Camera:** very high 21 mm aerial, level horizon; beach sweeping along the lower third.
- **Typography:** two-line lockup, lower-left: **INSPIRED BY THE OPEN-WORLD GENRE.** (white) / **BUILT AS OUR OWN WORLD.** (gold).
- **Notes:** the widest "scale" frame — emphasize believable human and building scale.

## BANNER 10 — THE FUTURE OF BLOCKSIDE (pairs with Tweet 10)

- **Visual:** the most impressive artwork of the campaign — sunset-to-blue-hour panorama; sun touching the ocean horizon on the left; grand boulevard with early-evening traffic and the first light streaks; an original art-deco landmark tower silhouetted against the sunset; city lights igniting building by building.
- **Camera:** elevated 35 mm, classic key-art composition; skyline upper-right third.
- **Typography:** headline **THE CITY IS YOURS.** large, lower-center-left; subtitle **FOLLOW THE JOURNEY.** in gold beneath; optional handle line (replace with the real @handle).
- **Notes:** doubles as the X profile header crop and the finale video poster frame; the art-deco tower becomes BLOCKSIDE's recurring landmark/identity device.
