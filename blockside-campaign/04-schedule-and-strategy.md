# BLOCKSIDE — Posting Order, 10-Day Schedule & Engagement Strategy

## Recommended posting order

Post 1 → 10 exactly as numbered. The arc alternates "world" posts and "systems" posts and lands on the emotional finale:

**announce → world → you → drive → living city → time → weather → roadmap → inspiration → vision**

- **Craft:** `#gameart` `#conceptart`

- **Rule:** max 3 hashtags per post. Secondary campaign tag for Days 1 & 10: `#TheCityIsYours`

## Engagement playbook

1. **First 120 minutes:** reply to every comment; like/RT good replies — early velocity drives distribution.
2. **Quote-tweet (don't reply) your own posts** the next day with one new detail — doubles feed presence without looking spammy.
3. **Polls on Days 4 & 10** convert lurkers into participants; publicly commit to acting on results.
4. **Reply templates:**
   - "Is this a GTA clone?" → "Original IP — inspired by the genre's ambition, building our own city and universe. No Rockstar assets anywhere."
   - "When can I play?" → "In development — we share progress weekly. Follow the page and you'll see it first."
   - "What engine?" → answer honestly per your actual stack.
5. **Between banner days (optional):** one short WIP clip post per week — motion outperforms static art.
6. **Honesty rule:** never label concept art as gameplay; keep the "IN DEVELOPMENT" badge in all art.
7. **Cross-post:** 1:1 crops to TikTok/IG/Discord; keep X posts native and self-contained.
8. **Measure per post:** impressions, profile visits, follows, replies. Double down on the top two themes in week 2 (expect vehicles + weather to win).
